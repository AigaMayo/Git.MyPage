EXPERIENCE / THE JOURNEY SO FAR
2025– / Joint Research on MEMS Gyroscopes

  • Developing real-time mismatch compensation for frequency-modulated and integrating MEMS gyroscopes.
  • Developing automatic optimization of electrostatic tuning coefficients in ring MEMS gyroscopes to address ultra-low-frequency drift.

2025 / Life Is Tech DACCHA! — DX Accelerator Creation Challenge

2025 / SEMICON Japan Student Ideathon

  • Presented on the future of semiconductors and autonomous robots, exploring a shift from being observed to communicating.
  • Received the Rapidus Award.

2021–2025 / From-The-Earth

  • Developed a J-class hybrid rocket engine (2021–2025).
  • Developed and launched Mikan, a K-class hybrid rocket (2023–2024).
  • Developed a CanSat (2023–2024).
  • Developed and launched Tanpopo, an I-class hybrid rocket (2022–2023).
  • Developed 12AY, an L-class hybrid rocket (2021–2022).
  • Developed and launched Tsubomi, a J-class hybrid rocket (2021–2022).
  • Developed and launched Wakaba, an F-class composite-propellant rocket (2021).

2024 / WOW!BASE Social Media Marketing Program

  • A program with the Cabinet Office Cool Japan initiative and FinT, sharing the appeal of Japan by understanding international audiences.

2024 / WOW!BASE Cool Japan Video Program & Cabinet Office Policy Discussion

  • Created videos showcasing Obama, Fukui, through the WOW!BASE program at Recruit.
  • Discussed Cool Japan policy at the Cabinet Office Intellectual Property Strategy Promotion Office.

2023 / Kyoto University Space Medicine Program at Tokushima University Hospital

  • Selected for a MEXT-funded aerospace science and technology program for the second time.
  • Studied ISS utilization policy and muscle atrophy in orbit at Tokushima University Hospital.
  • Completed the Kyoto University course on human space medicine.

2023 / Ryman-Sat-Project

  • Joined RymanSatSpaces, a community making space development accessible to everyone.

2020–2022 / Seek-Sole-Self Student Organization

  • Co-founded a student organization for high school students, helping them pursue a life doing what they love.
  • Focused on defining the founding principles and organizational structure.

2020 / The Global Enterprise Challenge

  • An international competition to develop a business proposal within 12 hours, addressing global challenges in the environment, food, energy, industry, disaster preparedness, education, and welfare.

2019–2020 / Tokyo University of Science Space Education Program

  • Selected for a MEXT-funded aerospace science and technology program for the first time.
  • Attended courses and lectures on space engineering, astrophysics, astronomy, space environment utilization, and space business.
  • Developed a 360-degree monitoring system for future Mars exploration (2019).
  • Designed an experiment to visualize viscous fluid behavior in microgravity and developed automated equipment (2019–2020).

2017–2019 / School Science Club

  • Researched resistance to penicillin antibiotics in soil bacteria.
  • Researched pH dependence in nonlinear oscillations of the Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction.
